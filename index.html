Preserve the evidence

Source snapshots, timestamps and provenance are the foundation. An extracted observation is not automatically universal truth.

Resolve identity carefully

Companies, products and relationships need durable identities. Ambiguity and conflicting claims must remain visible.

Track meaningful change

The goal is to identify changes in markets and products, not merely changed HTML, and trace each signal back to evidence.

First proving target

One market. One hundred companies.

Market Graph will connect companies, products, pricing pages, announcements and other supported public observations in one bounded market. The first task is to prove repeatable acquisition, identity, provenance and history before expanding coverage.

What exists today: the Studio / Lab demonstrates deterministic analysis, revisions and evidence. The web acquisition and Market Graph product are being built; live market coverage, monitoring subscriptions and graph APIs are not yet available.

The Lab stays. The mission grows.

DataGraph Studio remains our demonstration and future bring-your-own-data workbench. It is part of the foundry’s toolkit, not the definition of the company.
