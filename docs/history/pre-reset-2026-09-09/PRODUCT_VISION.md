# Product vision

## The company

DataGraphs turns data into inspectable, reproducible, versioned intelligence for humans and software. It addresses a widening gap: generation is cheap, but analysis is not trustworthy when schema, metric, filter, join, grain, currency, or time interpretation is wrong.

The initial beneficiary is a commerce founder/operator with CSV exports and consequential questions but no appetite for BI infrastructure. DataGraphs wins when that person can get a correct answer quickly and can prove where it came from.

## Product principles

1. AI proposes intent; deterministic systems compute values.
2. Source, semantics, computation, evidence, and presentation are distinct layers.
3. Material outputs carry a reconstruction path.
4. Plans are constrained and validated; customer execution never runs arbitrary model code.
5. Unknown stays unknown; material ambiguity blocks or is qualified.
6. Provenance and version history are product features.
7. The DataGraph—not the transcript or chart—is persistent.

## Mature platform

Studio provides human exploration and communication. Engine runs explicit safe operations. The Semantic Layer governs reusable entities, dimensions, metrics, units, and relationships. Provenance connects questions through results and claims. Signals rerun the same objects against changing data. API, SDK, embeds, and MCP eventually let agents request governed answers without unrestricted database access.

We explicitly defer broad connectors, joins, dashboards, collaboration, alerts, enterprise governance, agent access, and billing until the human-facing trust loop earns them.
